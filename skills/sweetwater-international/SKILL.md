# Sweetwater outside the US

- Sweetwater's international shipping is limited item by item by manufacturer restrictions. The help pages give no country list and tell you to contact them. Sources: `https://www.sweetwater.com/help/shipping-delivery.php` and `https://www.sweetwater.com/insync/faq-sweetwater-ship-outside-of-usa/`, read via Exa on 2026-11-20. Re-read them before advising.
- Phone: +1 800 222-4700. Answers from a Sales Engineer are the authority on a specific item and country.
- Gear Exchange used items are often local pickup only, so they are out of reach for buyers abroad.

## Steps for an international buyer

1. Price the item with `sweetwater-listing-check`, carrying the cache warning.
2. Price the same item at a dealer that does ship abroad, e.g. B&H with the `bh-photo` plugin.
3. Add a third option: delivery to a US address the user controls (a friend or a forwarder), then the destination's import rules.
4. Report the three side by side. Mark anything not confirmed, such as Sweetwater export eligibility or shipping cost.
